Workplace Safety

Workplace Safety & Accident Prevention

Spot hazards before they become accidents — slips, burns, cuts, fires and manual handling — in a full-day, hands-on session on your own floor.

Enquire about this course

Course overview

F&B kitchens concentrate more hazards per square metre than almost any other workplace: hot oil, sharp blades, wet floors, gas, heavy stock. This full-day course trains your team to see those hazards, control them and respond when something goes wrong — using your own equipment and layout, not classroom photos.

What you will learn

  • Identify common F&B workplace hazards: slips and trips, burns and scalds, cuts, fire and gas
  • Apply safe manual-handling technique for stock, pots and deliveries
  • Use knives, slicers and hot equipment with the correct guards and technique
  • Carry out basic risk assessment and hazard reporting for their work area
  • Respond to accidents: first response, escalation and incident recording

Who should attend

All F&B staff; strongly recommended for kitchen crew and anyone handling hot equipment or deliveries.

Food Safety and Hygiene (Internal SOP Training)

Ground your food handlers in safe, hygienic practice — from personal hygiene to temperature control — mapped directly to your outlet's internal SOP.

Enquire about this course

Course overview

This half-day session covers the food safety and hygiene fundamentals every food handler must apply daily, taught against your own kitchen's internal SOP rather than a generic textbook. Learners practise on the equipment, storage areas and prep stations they actually use, so the standards stick after the trainer leaves.

What you will learn

  • Apply correct personal hygiene and grooming standards before and during food handling
  • Prevent cross-contamination between raw and ready-to-eat food
  • Receive, store and thaw food at safe temperatures with correct labelling and FIFO rotation
  • Clean and sanitise food-contact surfaces and equipment to SOP standard
  • Identify and report food safety hazards and near-misses to the supervisor

Who should attend

Food handlers, kitchen assistants, prep cooks and new joiners in any F&B outlet.

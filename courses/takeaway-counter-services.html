Takeaway and Counter Services

Share ★★★★★ Review us on Google

Serve, pack and hand over takeaway orders accurately and hygienically — counter service, packing standards and queue handling.

📘 AMFC-TC01 ⏱️ 8 hours (1 day) 💰 Free (Pro-Bono)

What this course is about

Takeaway and counter service is where most customers actually meet the business, and where mistakes are most visible: the wrong order, a leaking container, a cold meal, a queue that stops moving. This full-day course is delivered pro-bono to food court stalls and small operators, and covers the counter operation end to end at your own premises. Learners work on order taking and accuracy, packing standards that keep food at temperature and intact in transit, correct labelling for both walk-in and delivery-platform orders, and hygienic handling at the point of handover. The session also covers the customer-facing side — greeting, handling a queue under pressure, and dealing with a complaint without escalating it — plus the halal segregation that packing and shared utensils must respect.

Course objectives

By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

  • Take and confirm orders accurately, including delivery-platform orders
  • Pack takeaway orders to keep food at temperature, intact and correctly separated
  • Label orders correctly, including allergen, halal and delivery information
  • Handle food hygienically at the counter and point of handover
  • Manage a queue under pressure while maintaining service standards
  • Handle customer complaints and order errors calmly and correctly

Who should attend

Counter and takeaway crew at food court stalls, kiosks and small outlets, including part-time and new service staff.

Modes of training

  • Classroom / on-site facilitated
  • Practical hands-on at your counter

Course fees and funding

This course is delivered pro-bono — free of charge — including all training materials and the certificate of attendance.

Funding validity period: Not applicable — delivered pro-bono

Facilities & equipment used to conduct training

Training is delivered at your premises. The following facilities and equipment are used to conduct this course:

  • Client's own service counter and takeaway station, used as the training floor
  • Portable projector, screen and laptop brought on site for the theory segment
  • Flip chart, whiteboard and printed learner guides for each participant
  • Takeaway containers, lids, bags and labels for the packing practice
  • Sample order tickets and delivery-platform labels for the accuracy exercise
  • Handwashing station, sanitiser and glove stock for the hygiene segment
